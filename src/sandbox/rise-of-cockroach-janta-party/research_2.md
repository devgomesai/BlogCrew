# Research Notes: The Catalyst, Offline Mobilization, and Legislative Frameworks

## Key Insights
* The drowning of three UPSC aspirants on July 27, 2024, in the basement library of Rau's IAS Study Circle in Old Rajinder Nagar became the primary catalyst for student safety protests in Delhi, exposing severe municipal corruption and structural safety violations in coaching hubs. | https://www.bbc.com/news/articles/c0xjlll8l24o | Core catalyst of the Delhi student safety protests.
* Gen Z student mobilization in Delhi underwent a major transformation from online satire (memes and parody) to physical street protests, exemplified by the Cockroach Janta Party (CJP) which turned internet parody into physical rallies at Jantar Mantar. | https://www.bbc.com/news/articles/cz72y11jjq1o | Illustrates the transition from digital satire to real-world street politics.
* The NEET-UG exam paper leak scandal served as a massive grievance point, prompting thousands of students to march on the streets of Delhi, Jantar Mantar, and near Parliament, clashing with police and triggering nationwide solidarity rallies across 10 states. | https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5sCX_qSybic | Examines the turnouts, demonstration locations, and impact of the NEET paper leak crisis.
* Student-led protests in Delhi successfully forced policy responses, including the sealing of illegal basements by the MCD and the announcement of a student-official committee by Delhi's government to draft a law regulating coaching center safety and infrastructure. | https://thefederal.com/category/news/neet-paper-leaks-students-protest-across-india-251007 | Examines the legislative and regulatory outcomes of student mobilization.
* Extreme rent inflation in Delhi's student hubs forces economically vulnerable students to reside in windowless, highly hazardous basement PGs (which cost half as much as above-ground rooms), highlighting the critical student demand for rent control legislation. | https://www.thehindu.com/news/cities/Delhi/find-it-difficult-to-breathe-but-cant-afford-rooms-above-the-basement/article68462052.ece | Directly links rent inflation to student safety hazards.
* A massive regulatory vacuum exists in Delhi's student housing sector, with over 2 lakh PG accommodations operating in student hubs like Mukherjee Nagar, Laxmi Nagar, and Rajendra Nagar without municipal registry, fire safety clearances, or police records. | https://www.nationalheraldindia.com/national/alarming-neglect-over-2-lakh-pgs-in-delhi-live-in-unregulated-ghettos | Explains the systemic regulatory gap in student-dominated residential areas.

## Named Examples
* **Rau's IAS Study Circle**: The Old Rajinder Nagar coaching institute where the flooded basement library tragedy on July 27, 2024, killed 3 students due to structural and municipal neglect. | https://www.bbc.com/news/articles/c0xjlll8l24o | High-profile coaching center named as the epicenter of student safety protests.
* **The Cockroach Janta Party (CJP)**: A satirical political movement founded on May 16, 2026, by digital strategist Abhijeet Dipke in response to comments by Chief Justice Surya Kant, which successfully transitioned from online memes to major real-world protests in Delhi. | https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cockroach_Janta_Party | Named example of online-to-offline protest mobilization.
* **Jantar Mantar**: The prominent protest location in central New Delhi where hundreds of students in cockroach masks and carrying books gathered in June/July 2026 to demand Dharmendra Pradhan's resignation. | https://www.aljazeera.com/amp/gallery/2026/6/6/youth-led-satire-cockroach-janta-party-protests-exam-system-failures | Named primary location for youth-led and CJP-led demonstrations.
* **Sonam Wangchuk**: Prominent environmental activist who went on an indefinite hunger strike starting June 28, 2026, at Jantar Mantar in solidarity with the CJP and student-led NEET paper leak protests. | https://www.deccanherald.com/india/woman-hurls-ink-like-liquid-at-cjp-founder-abhijeet-dipke-at-jantar-mantar-4078284 | Named example of seasoned activist support for student movement.
* **Mukherjee Nagar, Laxmi Nagar, and Rajendra Nagar**: Prominent student ghettos and coaching hubs in Delhi characterized by massive density, wooden/plastic room partitions, and completely unregulated PG housing. | https://www.nationalheraldindia.com/national/alarming-neglect-over-2-lakh-pgs-in-delhi-live-in-unregulated-ghettos | Named geographic areas in Delhi under high risk of student housing safety failures.
