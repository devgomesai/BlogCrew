### Section 1: Genesis of the Cockroach Janta Party (CJP)

#### Key Insights
- Satirical online phenomenon turned street-level protest | The Cockroach Janta Party (CJP) began as a digital satirical protest against political classism on May 16, 2026, and rapidly mobilized into India's largest youth-led street protest at Jantar Mantar by June 2026. | https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cockroach_Janta_Party | Connects the online origins of the CJP to its transformation into a major physical movement.
- Reclaiming of an elitist slur | The "cockroach" symbol was reclaimed by Indian youth after Chief Justice Surya Kant on May 15, 2026, compared unemployed youth and social media activists to "cockroaches" and "parasites." | https://www.bbc.com/news/articles/cz72y11jjq1o | Explains the catalyst and meaning behind the cockroach symbol.
- Indestructibility and coaching hub misery | The cockroach symbol represents the indestructible resilience and squalid living conditions of middle-class UPSC and SSC aspirants residing in cramped, illegal, windowless basement rooms of Delhi's coaching hubs (e.g., Old Rajinder Nagar and Mukherjee Nagar). | https://m.thewire.in/article/rights/cockroach-janta-party-student-movement-transformations | Explains why the insect became a highly personal and powerful satirical representation for Delhi’s coaching-hub students.
- Non-violent accountability focus over regime change | Unlike youth uprisings in Bangladesh, Sri Lanka, or Nepal, the CJP strictly practices non-violence and focuses on a narrow democratic demand—the resignation of Union Education Minister Dharmendra Pradhan over the 2026 NEET paper leaks—rather than general regime change. | https://www.thesaturdaypaper.com.au/news/politics/2026/07/11/rise-indias-cockroach-janta-party | Details CJP's strategic choice to remain within a peaceful, constitutional framework.

#### Statistics (2026 + Source)

### Section 2: Traditional Student Politics vs. CJP

#### Key Insights
- Rejection of money and muscle politics | Modern students bypassed traditional student unions like ABVP and NSUI because university student politics (e.g., Delhi University Student Union/DUSU) has become dominated by elite, NCR landlord classes, massive financial spending, and violent, caste-based social engineering rather than student welfare. | https://frontline.thehindu.com/news/dusu-election-abvp-nsui-aisa-sfi-womens-participation-caste-money-power/article68688969.ece | Explains student disillusionment with the money and power driving traditional unions.
- Institutional complicity and silence of ABVP | Party-affiliated student unions like ABVP are tied directly to mainstream political organizations (such as the ruling BJP). Consequently, during systemic failures like the 2026 NEET exam paper leak, ABVP is seen as toothless or complicit because they cannot demand the resignation of their parent party's union ministers (like Dharmendra Pradhan). | https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_Delhi_Jantar_Mantar_protests | Details the failure of party-aligned unions to hold state power accountable.
- CJP's decentralized digital-first model vs. Top-down structures | Modern Gen Z students bypassed traditional top-down union structures in favor of CJP's horizontal, meme-driven, low barrier-to-entry digital spaces (primarily Instagram and X) which weaponize self-deprecating humor and satire. | https://m.thewire.in/article/rights/cockroach-janta-party-student-movement-transformations | Contrasts CJP's engagement model with traditional unions.
- Non-ideological "Big Tent of Dissent" | While Left-wing unions like AISA/SFI joined Jantar Mantar, students flocked to CJP because it did not require aligning with rigid political ideologies (Marxism or Hindutva), creating a safe "big tent" for marginalized groups like Dalit and Muslim students. | https://m.thewire.in/article/rights/cockroach-janta-party-student-movement-transformations | Highlights how the absence of partisan branding fostered a safer environment for diverse students.

#### Statistics (2026 + Source)
- Over 25 million online supporters bypassing traditional channels (2026) | Rather than joining established campus wings, over 25 million Gen Z youth chose to affiliate online with the non-registered, satirical CJP, illustrating a major shift away from traditional student bodies. | https://www.thesaturdaypaper.com.au/news/politics/2026/07/11/rise-indias-cockroach-janta-party | Sourced proof of students shifting allegiance from established structures.
- Double student enrollment without infrastructure scaling (2026) | Mainstream campus infrastructure is collapsing under a two-fold student enrollment increase caused by the Four Year Undergraduate Programme (FYUP) without added funding, leading to student anger over collapsing ceilings and packed classrooms. | https://theprint.in/ground-reports/delhi-university-is-crumbling-and-it-has-united-nsui-abvp-aisa/2739779 | Demonstrates why academic anger boiled over into bypassing traditional administrative channels.
